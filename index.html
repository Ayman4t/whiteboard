The host stays in control

The host decides who joins. They can set people to view-only, turn off chat, mute a mic, or remove someone from the room. Your board is also saved on your device for when you come back.

About Inkroom

Inkroom is a shared infinite whiteboard that lives in a single web page. It was built so a group can start sketching together in seconds, without accounts, installs or setup.

Drawings travel directly between the browsers in your room, and the host decides who joins. Whether it is a team brainstorm, a lesson on a chalkboard or an engineering sketch, Inkroom keeps the canvas simple and fast.
