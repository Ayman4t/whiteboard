SketchMesh is a shared whiteboard for teams, classrooms and friends. Make a room, send a link, and everyone sketches at the same time, on a clean whiteboard or a classic chalkboard.

Everything a meeting needs on a board

Sketch, explain, mark up a photo and talk it through, all in one place.

Real drawing tools

Draw freehand, highlight, erase, or drop in shapes. Pick any color, set the stroke size, fill shapes, and undo or redo as you go.

PenHighlighterEraserRectangleCircleSticky notes

Infinite canvas

Move around freely and zoom in on details. It works with a mouse, a trackpad or touch.

Rooms with a link

Create a room, copy the invite link, and friends join in one click. A live list shows who is in the room.

Photo markup

Upload a photo and explain right on top of it.

Video and chat

Start a video call or send messages without leaving the board.

The host stays in control

The host decides who joins. They can set people to view-only, turn off chat, mute a mic, or remove someone from the room. Your board is also saved on your device for when you come back.

Your board, your look

Switch between whiteboard and chalkboard, change the background color, and choose a grid style.

Save as an image

Export the board as a PNG, with its background or transparent, ready for slides and documents.

The classic chalkboard, back on screen

Teach, explain and calculate on a green or black board. Set what each grid square means, snap lines to the grid, and place standard symbols you can rotate and resize.

From blank board to shared in three steps

Create a room

Press Create room on this page. The whiteboard opens with a new room code, or you can keep working alone.

Share the link

Copy the invite link. Others join with it or with the room code, and the host lets them in.

Draw and talk

Sketch at the same time, chat, switch on video, then save the result as an image.
